## 🏆 Leaderboards & Performance Metrics

### [Scale Labs Leaderboard](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard)

**Primary Use:** Testing the limits of AI through expert-driven benchmarks.

#### Focus
High-complexity evaluations that target agentic workflows, reasoning, and safety.

#### Key Benchmarks
- **SWE Atlas**  
  Measures coding capabilities across refactoring, codebase Q&A, and test writing.

- **Professional Reasoning**  
  Specialized evaluations for:
  - Finance
  - Legal

- **HiL-Bench**  
  Tests Human-in-the-Loop capabilities by evaluating whether an AI knows when to stop and ask for clarification.

#### Best For
Selecting models for:
- Autonomous agents
- Specialized professional environments
- Advanced reasoning systems

---

### [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/)

**Primary Use:** Independent, data-driven comparison of models and API providers.

#### Focus
A "Consumer Reports" style platform focused on:
- Speed
- Cost
- Quality

#### Key Metrics
- **Intelligence Index**  
  Composite score based on reasoning and knowledge benchmarks.

- **Speed vs. Price**  
  Real-time tracking of:
  - Tokens per second (TPS)
  - Cost per 1M tokens

  Across providers such as:
  - OpenAI
  - Anthropic
  - Groq
  - Others

- **Quality Breakdown**  
  Detailed analysis of:
  - Knowledge accuracy
  - Hallucination rates

#### Best For
- CTOs optimizing infrastructure costs
- Developers scaling AI products
- Production benchmarking and ROI analysis

## 📈 Research & Forecasting

### [METR: Measuring AI Time Horizons](https://metr.org/blog/2025-03-19-measuring-ai-ability-to-complete-long-tasks/)

**Primary Use:** Forecasting the future of AI task completion.

#### Focus
Measures AI performance based on the **length and complexity of tasks** an agent can complete autonomously.

#### Core Finding
Agentic capabilities are doubling approximately every **7 months**.

#### Best For
Understanding:
- AI capability growth trajectories
- Long-term automation potential
- When AI may handle multi-day or multi-week human projects
